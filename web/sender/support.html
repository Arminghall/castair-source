Where do I find the share code?

When laptop sharing is on, the TV shows a 4-digit code next to its address, castair.app/connect. Type it on the page when you pick the TV. The code changes every 10 minutes and after each share ends, so use the one on screen now. If you type the wrong code several times, that TV makes your network wait a minute before you can try again.

Why did my cast stop after 5 minutes?

The free version of CastAir casts for 5 minutes at a time, with a warning at 4 minutes. Start another cast any time, or unlock unlimited casts with a monthly or yearly plan, or a one-off lifetime purchase, in CastAir's Settings on the TV, under CastAir Unlimited. The app shows the prices.

I've bought Unlimited but the TV still says free

On the TV, open Settings, then CastAir Unlimited, and choose Check purchases again. This is also how a purchase made on another TV on the same Amazon account shows up.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Amazon. Cancel or change them in your Amazon account under Memberships & Subscriptions. They renew until cancelled.

The TV isn't listed on castair.app/connect, but we're on the same Wi-Fi

CastAir 1.0 doesn't support homes or offices that mix IPv4 and IPv6, where the laptop and the TV reach the internet in different ways, so they don't see each other as being on one network. You can:

  • Use Screen Mirroring from an iPhone, iPad or Mac instead, which doesn't need the website; or
  • Connect the laptop to the same network the TV uses: turn off a VPN, or use the TV's Wi-Fi rather than a phone hotspot or Ethernet on a different network.

Which browsers can share a screen?

Chrome and Edge on a laptop. On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, use Screen Mirroring in Control Center.
