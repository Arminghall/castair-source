Help

Quick answers to what people ask most. The TV shows the steps too: press a round button on its standby screen.

How do I share from an iPhone, iPad or Mac?

Join the same Wi-Fi as the TV. Open Control Centre, choose Screen Mirroring, then choose the TV. Its name starts with CastAir. No website or app needed.

How do I share from a laptop?

Open castair.app in Chrome or Edge, pick the TV, type the code it shows and choose Share screen. You can share your whole screen, one window or a browser tab.

Where do I find the share code?

When laptop sharing is on, the TV shows a 4-digit code under its address, castair.app. The code changes every 10 minutes and after each share ends, so use the one on screen now. If you type the wrong code several times, that TV makes your network wait a minute before you can try again.

The TV isn't listed, but we're on the same Wi-Fi

Check the TV is on and shows CastAir is ready, and turn off any VPN on the laptop. The page looks for TVs again every few seconds.

CastAir 1.0 doesn't support networks that mix IPv4 and IPv6, where the laptop and the TV reach the internet in different ways and so don't see each other as being on one network. You can:

  • Use Screen Mirroring from an iPhone, iPad or Mac instead, which doesn't need the website; or
  • Connect the laptop to the same network the TV uses, rather than a phone hotspot or Ethernet on a different network.
Why did my cast stop?

The free version gives you 30 minutes of casting every week. The TV warns you with 5 minutes and again with 1 minute left, and the time refills on Monday at 05:00.

To cast without a limit, open Settings on the TV and choose CastAir Unlimited. There's a monthly or yearly plan, or a one-off lifetime purchase. The app shows the prices.

How do I share from Windows with Win+K?

Windows sharing comes with CastAir Unlimited. On the TV, press the Windows button on the standby screen, then Enable Windows sharing. On the laptop press Win+K and choose the Fire TV. Press Back on the remote to return to CastAir.

Any laptop can also share from castair.app in Chrome or Edge.

I've bought Unlimited but the TV still says free

On the TV, open Settings, then CastAir Unlimited, and choose Check purchases again. This is also how a purchase made on another TV on the same Amazon account shows up.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Amazon. Cancel or change them in your Amazon account under Memberships & Subscriptions. They renew until cancelled.

Which browsers can share a screen?

Chrome and Edge on a laptop. Phones and tablets can't share from a browser; on an iPhone or iPad, use Screen Mirroring in Control Centre.
