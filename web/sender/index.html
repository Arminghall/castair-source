    Share from an iPhone, iPad or Mac

    1. Join the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
    2. Open Control Centre and choose Screen Mirroring.
    3. Choose the TV. Its name is on the TV, starting with CastAir.

    No website needed. It works the same way as on any AirPlay TV.

    Can't see your TV?

    1. Check the TV is on and showing CastAir is ready.
    2. Join the same Wi-Fi as the TV, and turn off any VPN.
    3. Give it a few seconds. This page looks again by itself.

    Still nothing? Read the help page, or email castair@arminghall.com.
