Three ways in, all from the device you already have.

The TV shows how, too: each round button on its standby screen opens the steps.

AirPlay

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Open Control Centre, choose Screen Mirroring and pick the TV. Picture and sound, with no app to add.

Cast from a browser

Any laptop, in Chrome or Edge

Go to castair.app, pick the TV, type the code it shows and share a screen, a window or a tab.

Windows

Windows laptops

Choose Enable Windows sharing on the TV, then press Win+K on the laptop and pick the Fire TV.
