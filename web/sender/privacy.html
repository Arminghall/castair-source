Privacy

24 November 2026

CastAir is built to keep what you share to yourself. This page says exactly what it does with data.

What you mirror

Your screen goes straight from your device to the TV, and nowhere else. It is never recorded, and it never passes through CastAir's servers. The connection is direct; our server only introduces the two devices to each other.

What CastAir's server receives

  • The TV's time zone, and the town you choose in the TV's Settings, to show the time and weather.
  • Device and TV names, and IP addresses, used only to connect devices on the same network (a laptop is only listed TVs on its own network).

The server holds the time zone and town only while it answers the request, and device and TV names and IP addresses only while the device is connected. To protect against abuse it also keeps two short-lived records: a count of recent connection offers per network (your IP address, or the first half of an IPv6 address, with timestamps) for about 10 minutes after the last one, and a count of wrong share codes for about 2 minutes. CastAir's server writes nothing to disk and logs none of this. Cloudflare and the hosting provider may keep standard request logs.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Amazon. CastAir's server never sees them.

Wallpapers

If you turn wallpapers on, the TV loads the photos directly from Unsplash.

Analytics

CastAir has no analytics and sends no crash reports.
